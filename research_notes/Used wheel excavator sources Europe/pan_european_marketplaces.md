# Pan-European marketplaces and aggregators listing used wheel excavators (checked 2026-11-07)

Evidence labels used throughout:
- **[FETCHED]** the category page itself was read on 2026-11-07, so the counts are live page values.
- **[SNIPPET]** the site blocked or failed the fetch, and the figure comes only from a search-engine result of unknown age. Treat it as indicative.
- **[BLOCKED]** nothing beyond the fact of the block could be verified.

Counts are for the category as shown. They usually include new machines, auctions and some non-European stock, unless noted.

## Which marketplaces actually list used wheel excavators, and what does each offer?

### Takeaway
Six independent listing databases were verified live: Mascus, Machineryline/Autoline, Truck1, Europe-Construction-Equipment, TruckScout24 and Maschinensucher/Machineseeker. MachineryZone is very probably a seventh large one, but it could only be seen through search snippets. Mascus (about 3,315) and Machineryline (2,334 worldwide, 1,728 Europe) are the largest. Newly discovered sources (Cyclica, Equippo, Landwirt.com, Machinery Trader EU) are small, partly unverified, or mostly listed on the bigger portals as well.

#### Fees and caveats

- No buyer fee statement appeared on the Machineryline wheel excavator page — [Machineryline](https://machineryline.info/-/wheel-excavators--c164)
- Equippo's live auctions were reported in a search summary as unreserved with a 12% buyer's fee. This was not read on the page and applies to auctions, not fixed-price listings — [Equippo buying](https://www.equippo.com/en/buying/)
- TruckScout24 descriptions say some sales are "nur an Gewerbetreibende" (trade buyers only) — [TruckScout24](https://www.truckscout24.de/tssp/mobilbagger)
- The free-text Machineseeker search reports 21,362 "wheel excavator" results, with trucks and forklifts in its facets, so that number must not be used. The category figure of 118 is the valid one — [Keyword search](https://www.machineseeker.com/mss/wheel+excavator); [Category](https://www.machineseeker.com/Mobile-excavators/ci-866)

### Inferences
- Non-European stock must be removed from any Europe count. Removing China and Hong Kong leaves about 769 on Europe-Construction-Equipment (830 minus 61). Truck1 is at most about 1,036 (1,260 minus 224), and other non-European entries may remain.
- Mascus's header says "Graafmachines" (excavators), not wheel excavators. However, 83 pages at 40 per page fits 3,315, so it is probably the wheel excavator count.
- These are dealer-led sites. I saw no verified private sellers of wheel excavators on any of them, so buyers should expect commercial sellers and ex-VAT pricing.
- Machineryline's first-page Europe view is heavy with auction lots, so its count overstates fixed-price stock.
- Machinio's URLs with odd prefixes (/microtest/, /polytechnik/, /m/) look like auto-generated SEO pages, so they should not be counted as distinct inventory.

### Gaps
- Could not read Mascus's per-country or per-make counts, because none are displayed. The English mascus.com URL returned 403.
- Could not verify a current count, hours display or Hyundai filter on MachineryZone, Machinio, Machinery Trader EU, TradeMachines, Baupool, Exapro or Mobile.de.
- Buyer fee terms were not found for most portals. I found no evidence that classified portals charge buyers, but I did not read their terms, so this is unconfirmed.
- Agriaffaires' construction sister site appears to be MachineryZone (see the next section). No other sister site was checked.
- Other candidates searched but not surfaced by any query, so unverified either way: TrucksNL, Lectura, Machinerypark and Mach4.

### Takeaway
All verified portals show 2026 activity. **Tradus appears to be gone: every tradus.com URL tried redirected to otomoto.pl**, though I found no announcement explaining it. Autoline's wheel excavator category redirects to Machineryline, so these are not separate sources. Maschinensucher, Machineseeker and Used-Machines belong to one group, and TruckScout24 appears linked to it too.

### Inferences
- **Do not add the counts together.** The families above overlap, and even independent portals share many of the same dealers' machines.
- A practical non-duplicating sweep would be Mascus, Machineryline, Truck1, Europe-Construction-Equipment, MachineryZone, TruckScout24 and Maschinensucher. Autoline, Machineseeker and Used-Machines add little or nothing on top. Used-Machines' count is slightly higher than Maschinensucher's (127 vs 118), so it adds at most a few machines.
- Tradus should be treated as no longer usable. The fetcher appeared to be US-based (Machineseeker detected a US visitor), so a location-dependent redirect cannot be fully excluded, although it would be unusual for a 301 on every path.

### Gaps
- No primary announcement explains the Tradus redirect, and its current owner is unverified.
- Could not confirm TruckScout24's ownership, Truck1's owner, or whether Mascus's ownership has changed since 2016.
- Could not confirm that Planet-Trucks still runs as a site, or that Europe-TP and Europe-Construction-Equipment share one database (inferred from the shared operator).
- Could not confirm whether TradeMachines' HTTP 522 errors are temporary. It should not be called closed on this evidence.

- Yanmar is thin everywhere (4 to 14 listings where counted), so saved searches matter more for it than for other makes.
- Compare prices on an ex-VAT basis. Europe-Construction-Equipment states ex VAT, and Landwirt.com results were reported both with and without VAT.

- Saved-search or alert features were not checked on any portal.
