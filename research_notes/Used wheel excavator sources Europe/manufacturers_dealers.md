# Manufacturer programmes, official dealers and independent dealers for used wheel excavators in Europe (as of 2026-11-07)

Verification labels used throughout:
- **[F]** = the page itself was fetched in this research session. Page summaries were produced by a small extraction model, so counts should be re-checked by a human before publication.
- **[S]** = known only from a search-result summary. The page was not opened. Treat as lower confidence.
- **Blocked** = a fetch was attempted and failed.

Counts are what the page displayed on the fetch date. They are not Europe-only unless stated.

## Which manufacturers run official used machine portals or certified programmes in Europe?

### Takeaway
Liebherr, Volvo CE, Zeppelin (Cat), Hitachi, Komatsu, Develon and CASE all have an identifiable used channel, but wheel excavator stock was directly seen on only two manufacturer portals (Liebherr, Volvo CE). No official Europe-wide used portal was found for Hyundai, Yanmar, Takeuchi, Wacker Neuson or JCB; absence of a search hit is not proof that none exists.

**Liebherr**
- The Liebherr used machines page says listings come only from certified Liebherr sales and service partners, that machines "have been fully inspected", and that only traders can buy, with private purchases not possible — [Liebherr used machines](https://www.liebherr.com/en-int/group/about-liebherr/services/used-machines/used-machines-3935411) [F]
- The portal itself says inspection for earthmoving machines happens "at the customer's request" and is "not included in the standard offering" — [Liebherr Used Equipment Marketplace](https://used.liebherr.com/en/) [F]. This contradicts the "fully inspected" wording above; a buyer should ask per machine.
- The marketplace launched in 2024 — [Liebherr press release](https://liebherr.com/en/cze/latest-news/news-press-releases/detail/news_341632.html?page=16) [S]

**Volvo CE**
- From Bauma 2019, used Volvo machines in EMEA get an "Approved Used Equipment" sticker after inspection, and the warranty package depends on machine hours — [Volvo CE 2019](https://volvoce.com/europe/en/about-us/news/2019/volvo-approved-used-machines-just-as-reliable-as-new) [S]
- A 2015 EMEA brochure gives 1 year or 1,500 h for GPPE machines and 1 year or 1,000 h for compact and road machinery, parts only, labour excluded — [Volvo brochure](https://www.volvoce.com/-/media/volvoce/local/emea/shared/11-emea-approved-used/volvo-approved-used-brochure-a5-en-05-2015.pdf?v=dJAwPw) [S]. This is over a decade old.
- North American Volvo Certified terms do not apply to Europe — [Volvo brochure NA](https://www.volvoce.com/-/media/volvoce/local/americas/northamerica/pdfs/used-equipment/2020-new-certified-brochure-2.pdf) [S]

### Inferences
- For a buyer who wants terms in writing, Zeppelin's table is the most concrete of anything found. An M-series machine would likely fall under "standard and large", but that classification was not confirmed.
- Wheel excavators likely fall under Volvo's GPPE warranty line, though the brochure text seen does not say so.
- Yanmar's Mascus seller name uses the older company name; the company now presents as Yanmar Compact Equipment EMEA ([Yanmar about](https://www.yanmar.com/eu/construction/about_us/)). That listing feed may be stale.

### Gaps
- Portals not opened: useddevelon.eu, used.hitachicm.eu, used.komatsu.eu, catused.cat.com, yanmar.com. Wheel excavator stock on these needs a browser check.
- Liebherr and Volvo counts are global; no Europe-only count was obtained.
- No current Volvo Europe warranty terms found. Develon T&C document not retrieved.
- Pon: no stock page found. Bergerat Monnoyeur: no own-site used page verified. Finning: not verified.
- Not found does not mean nonexistent for Hyundai, Yanmar, Takeuchi, Wacker Neuson or JCB: dedicated searches were limited and yanmar.com was blocked.
