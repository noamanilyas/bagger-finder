# Auction platforms selling used wheel excavators in Europe (as of 2026-11-07)

Evidence labels used throughout:
- **[D]** = the page was fetched directly on 2026-11-07 and read through the fetch tool's summariser.
- **[V]** = the document text was read verbatim (Klaravik terms PDF only).
- **[S]** = seen only as text returned by the search tool for that URL. The direct fetch was blocked or not tried, so treat it as lower confidence.

Sites that refused fetching: euroauctions.com (403 on every page tried), rbauction.com and rbauction.co.uk (403; rbauction.eu worked), and help.surplex.com (403 on the costs page). ## Which auction houses and platforms sell wheel excavators in Europe?

### Takeaway
Three channels had wheel excavators confirmed in live category listings or a 2026 sale programme: Ritchie Bros (27 European lots), the TBAuctions group (Troostwijk 37 category lots, about 29 of them wheeled machines by title; Surplex 27, overlapping), and Euro Auctions (a dedicated wheeled-excavator ring at Dormagen in October 2026). Zoll-Auktion (Germany) has many past Mobilbagger lots from public bodies, and Klaravik and Auksjonen show wheel excavators in the Nordics. The other names are either unverified for this machine type or carried no fee data.

### Inferences
- A German-speaking or Benelux buyer has the most realistic coverage from RB, Euro Auctions and Troostwijk/Surplex, with Zoll-Auktion as an extra source of municipal machines.
- The real Troostwijk count is lower than the headline 37, so anyone tracking counts should filter by title.
- France's 17 lots make it RB's largest European pool of wheel excavators in this snapshot, but this is one day's data.

## What are the fees, VAT, inspection and bidding rules for each platform?

### Takeaway
Published scales exist only for RB and Euro Auctions, and both are low in percentage terms on mid-priced machines. The TBAuctions brands show the premium per lot, so it cannot be known in advance, and the only figures found are dated or from other sectors. Klaravik's own terms allow foreign companies to register but not foreign private individuals, and Surplex's terms are B2B only.

### Cited Findings

**Ritchie Bros (DE, FR, IT, NL, ES and others)**
- Buyer fee for events opening on or after 1 Aug 2026, Europe except UK: 15% up to EUR 5,500 (minimum EUR 100); 10% from EUR 5,500 to 13,500 (minimum EUR 825); 4.85% from EUR 13,500 to 79,000 (minimum EUR 1,350); flat EUR 3,850 above EUR 79,000. — [RB buyer fees, D](https://www.rbauction.eu/buying/buyer-fees)
- UK: 10% up to GBP 6,500, then GBP 650 plus 2.35% above that. — [same page, D](https://www.rbauction.eu/buying/buyer-fees)
- Documentation fee per titled item: DE and NL EUR 40 for foreign buyers, plus EUR 15 for German- or Dutch-registered items; FR EUR 40; IT EUR 132 for foreign buyers; ES none. Storage: EUR 25 a day in Europe, GBP 15 a day in the UK. All are plus taxes. — [same page, D](https://www.rbauction.eu/buying/buyer-fees)
- Note: excerpts from other RB domains showed a first tier ending at EUR 5,000, probably the earlier schedule. Use the August 2026 table. — [rbauction.com excerpt, S](https://www.rbauction.com/buying/buyer-fees)
- Deposit: 25% of expected purchases or EUR 10,000, whichever is higher, for on-site bidders; online bidders may be asked for the same depending on history. Cash not accepted. — [RB Europe deposits, D](https://www.rbauction.eu/heavy-equipment-auctions/europe-deposit-and-payment-information)
- Netherlands site terms: pay within 7 days, then 18% annual interest from day 8; remove within 2 weeks, with storage or third-party relocation possible after 3 weeks; non-EU export via a third-party shipper needs a deposit equal to local VAT, refunded within 10 business days of proof of export; "please inspect equipment before bidding". Whether consumers may bid is not stated. — [RB NL terms, D](https://www.rbauction.eu/legal-policies/site-specific-terms/netherlands-site-specific-terms)

**Euro Auctions (UK, DE, ES, IE)** (all [S]; direct fetch blocked)
- Commission per lot: 10% on the first GBP 4,500 and 2.5% on the balance, plus VAT. — [UK terms](https://www.euroauctions.com/en/help-support/policies/auction-terms-and-conditions/uk-terms-and-conditions). Germany and Spain use the same scale in euros. — [Germany](https://www.euroauctions.com/en/help-support/policies/auction-terms-and-conditions/germany-terms-and-conditions); [Spain](https://www.euroauctions.com/en/help-support/policies/auction-terms-and-conditions/spain-terms-and-conditions)
- An [older FAQ](http://www.euroauctions.com/Default.aspx?catid=4135) quotes a flat 2.5%; treat it as outdated.
- VAT, Germany: 19% on hammer plus commission for German buyers. EU buyers registered outside Germany are exempt if they prove export within 30 days. Non-EU buyers pay and can claim a refund with an original bill of lading. Spain follows the same pattern at 21%. UK: 20% on commission for all, with the same exemption and refund logic for the hammer price. — same terms pages
- Payment within 7 days, with interest at 4% above the central bank base rate. Removal within 2 weeks of the auction (UK terms), only after payment. Risk passes when the hammer falls; title passes on payment. — [UK terms](https://www.euroauctions.com/en/help-support/policies/auction-terms-and-conditions/uk-terms-and-conditions)
- Deposit sources differ: [registration page](https://www.euroauctions.com/en/buying/registration-requirements) gives a GBP 200 minimum and about 20% of the desired credit limit for new buyers; the [Germany and Spain terms](https://www.euroauctions.com/en/help-support/policies/auction-terms-and-conditions/germany-terms-and-conditions) give a standard EUR 15,000 for a EUR 50,000 limit.
- Business cards carry a 3% fee per the [payments page](https://www.euroauctions.com/en/buying/deposits-payment); the UK terms bar non-UK cards for final payment but allow them for deposits.
- Private buyers: registration asks for photo ID, plus a letter of authorisation if bidding for a company, which suggests individuals can register. No explicit statement was seen. — [Registration](https://www.euroauctions.com/en/buying/registration-requirements)

**Troostwijk**
- Premium is a percentage plus VAT that varies by country; some lots add a fixed service fee; VAT applies to bid, premium and service fees; a full breakdown appears before you confirm a bid. No rate is published. — [Help centre, D](https://service.troostwijkauctions.com/hc/en-us/articles/12076456121756-What-costs-are-added-to-my-bid)
- Only indications found: a seller-portal article gives 19% as the usual rate, with a pilot of about 8% and 13% on lots valued at EUR 10,000 or more (undated, and the rates are called "likely") — [Seller portal, D](https://sellerportal.troostwijkauctions.com/hc/en-us/articles/19605268405404-Pilot-variable-buyer-s-premium-promising-results). A Dutch medical-equipment sale showed 17% plus 21% VAT — [sale notice, S](https://api.troostwijkauctions.com/salefile/download/33568/0/157587?language=nl). Older Dutch terms showed 16% — [terms, S](https://api.troostwijkauctions.com/salefile/terms/30526/0/38?language=nl). None is specific to excavators.
- Dutch terms: pay within 48 hours of the payment notice (13.1); collect by the date set by Troostwijk (14.1); inspect at collection and report defects immediately (14.3, 6.2); sold in the condition at collection (16.2); auctions are public by default so withdrawal generally does not apply (12.1); default costs a fixed 25% of the winning bid plus premium (18.3). — [NL terms, D](https://www.troostwijkauctions.com/en/general-terms-and-conditions-netherlands)
- Group user terms: consumers buying from a professional seller must confirm the winning bid before a contract forms (5.2); deposit of at least EUR 200 is forfeited on non-payment (5.4); items are sold "with all their visible and invisible defects" (8.4). — [User terms, D](https://www.troostwijkauctions.com/uk/terms)

**Surplex**
- Terms apply only to entrepreneurs and public-law bodies; others need express consent, and registrants confirm they are not consumers (A.2(3), A.5(1)). Prices exclude VAT (A.4(1)). Default after 7 days from invoice (A.4(2)). Collect within one week unless otherwise agreed (C.3(4)). Second-hand items carry no warranty, apart from gross negligence, intent or injury (C.6(2)). Auction-specific terms can apply on top (A.1(2)). — [Surplex terms, D](https://www.surplex.com/en/terms-and-conditions)
- The premium percentage appears on the lot page and may vary by auction, project or category. — [Surplex help, S](https://help.surplex.com/hc/en-gb/articles/32290506054545-What-is-the-buyer-s-premium). Dismantling, packing or loading may cost extra. — [Costs help, S](https://help.surplex.com/hc/en-gb/articles/32290390771857-What-costs-do-I-need-to-take-into-account). I did not see an actual rate.

**Klaravik** (SE, DK, FI; a listing also mentions NO and PL)
- Registration is open to Swedish companies, Swedish private persons and foreign companies. Fee varies by object and shows only when logged in. Reserve prices are used, and a below-reserve bid stays binding for 2 working days. No warranty; no inspection by Klaravik; viewing normally not offered during the auction. Invoice due in 7 days. Pick-up within 12 days, then SEK 500 ex VAT per week; after 30 working days Klaravik may cancel and resell at the buyer's risk. Foreign companies pay VAT up front and must send export documents within 10 days of collection for a refund. Complaints must be made before the machine leaves the pick-up site. Consumers have 14 days to withdraw, but pay depreciation of SEK 1,200 per hour above 1 hour on machines over 5 t. — [Klaravik terms, V](https://www.klaravik.se/Terms_and_conditions.pdf) (document undated)
- Countries: [TBAuctions release on Finland](https://newsroom.tbauctions.com/posts/pressreleases/klaravik-expands-now-open-in-finland); [Truck1 dealer page, S](https://www.truck1.co.za/dealers/klaravik-ab)
- Trustpilot reviews include complaints about slow VAT refunds to international buyers. Anecdotal only. — [Trustpilot, S](https://uk.trustpilot.com/review/klaravik.se?page=4)

**Auksjonen.no**
- Buyer's auction fee varies by category and minimum price; no amount is published. — [Price list, D](https://www.auksjonen.no/infosenter/prisliste)
- Sold "som den er" and "hvor den er"; buyer should inspect at the location before bidding; payment due the business day after payment details are received, and more than 3 business days late is a material breach; collection within 3 business days of the pick-up document; storage from NOK 150 a day for motor vehicles; breach fee 10% for businesses (NOK 1,000 to 20,000) or NOK 1,500 for private buyers; consumers buying from a business have 14 days to withdraw, but the auction fee is not refunded. Private and business buyers are both catered for. — [Vilkår, D](https://www.auksjonen.no/infosenter/vilk%C3%A5r)

- Because Troostwijk premiums can be far higher than RB and Euro Auctions on expensive machines, the same hammer price is not the same cost across channels.
- A private individual in Germany should not rely on Surplex or Klaravik registration. Troostwijk's terms contemplate consumers, so it is the clearest option. RB, Euro Auctions and Zoll-Auktion need eligibility confirmed before bidding.

### Gaps
- No actual premium seen on a current Troostwijk, Surplex, Vavato, Klaravik or Auksjonen wheel excavator lot, because the pages hide it behind login or the lot URLs had expired.
- Vavato's terms were not retrieved (404 on the URL tried). IronPlanet's European fee was not found; it may or may not follow the RB table.
- RB's Germany-specific terms (Meppen) were blocked, so VAT and removal rules there are unverified. The NL terms should not be assumed to apply in Germany.
- Consumer eligibility at RB, Euro Auctions, VEBEG, NetBid and Zoll-Auktion is not explicitly confirmed.
- Euro Auctions' Dormagen and Leeds specifics rely on search excerpts and should be checked on the site by a human browser.

## Is each platform active in 2026, and what are the mergers and shared inventory?

### Takeaway
RB, Euro Auctions, Troostwijk, Surplex and Klaravik are clearly active. BVA Auctions is gone as a separate site, since its URL now redirects to Troostwijk. TBAuctions inventory is shared between brands, so the same machine can appear on several sites and must not be counted twice.

## What are the main risks when buying at auction?

### Takeaway
Every set of terms read puts condition risk on the buyer, usually with no warranty (mandatory consumer law can still apply, as Auksjonen's clause 5.1 notes), and the deadlines are short: payment in 1 to 7 days and collection in 3 business days to 2 weeks. Non-payment penalties can be severe, reaching 25% of the bid at Troostwijk NL. Cross-border buyers often pre-pay VAT and lose it if export proof is late.

### Cited Findings
- **As-is, no warranty:** RB "as is, where is" with no warranties, hour meter accuracy not verified — [RB NL, D](https://www.rbauction.eu/legal-policies/site-specific-terms/netherlands-site-specific-terms). Troostwijk "all their visible and invisible defects" — [User terms, D](https://www.troostwijkauctions.com/uk/terms). Klaravik "No warranty is given" — [Terms, V](https://www.klaravik.se/Terms_and_conditions.pdf). Surplex no warranty on second-hand items — [Terms, D](https://www.surplex.com/en/terms-and-conditions).
- **Limited or no inspection:** Klaravik does not inspect and normally offers no viewing during the auction; its description is interview-based. — [Terms, V](https://www.klaravik.se/Terms_and_conditions.pdf). Surplex lots are described as used and uninspected unless noted, per search excerpts of its lot pages (exact lot not recorded, so confirm on a live lot). [S]
- **Claims window closes at the gate:** Klaravik complaints must be made before the machine leaves the pick-up site, and a transport agent's collection counts as approval. — [Terms, V](https://www.klaravik.se/Terms_and_conditions.pdf). Troostwijk NL: report defects no later than collection. — [NL terms, D](https://www.troostwijkauctions.com/en/general-terms-and-conditions-netherlands). One forum user reported a hidden hydraulic fault with no remedy as a company buyer. — [SweClockers, S, anecdotal](https://www.sweclockers.com/forum/post/17507494)
- **Insolvency sellers:** Klaravik disclaims liability for items sold by bankruptcy managers. — [Terms, V](https://www.klaravik.se/Terms_and_conditions.pdf). Auksjonen says estate and creditor sellers disclaim hidden and obvious defects (5.2). — [Vilkår, D](https://www.auksjonen.no/infosenter/vilk%C3%A5r)
- **Tight deadlines and storage:** Troostwijk NL payment within 48 hours of notice; Auksjonen collection within 3 business days; Surplex one week; Klaravik 12 days; RB NL and Euro Auctions UK two weeks. RB storage is EUR 25 a day. — sources as cited in the previous section
- **Default costs:** Troostwijk NL 25% of bid plus premium and damages; RB NL 18% annual interest plus legal costs; Auksjonen up to NOK 20,000; Klaravik resale at the buyer's risk. — [Troostwijk](https://www.troostwijkauctions.com/en/general-terms-and-conditions-netherlands); [RB](https://www.rbauction.eu/legal-policies/site-specific-terms/netherlands-site-specific-terms); [Auksjonen](https://www.auksjonen.no/infosenter/vilk%C3%A5r); [Klaravik](https://www.klaravik.se/Terms_and_conditions.pdf)
- **Export and VAT paperwork:** Euro Auctions wants third-party proof of export within 30 days or VAT becomes due — [Germany terms, S](https://www.euroauctions.com/en/help-support/policies/auction-terms-and-conditions/germany-terms-and-conditions). Klaravik wants CMR, transporter invoice and its declaration within 10 days of collection — [Terms, V](https://www.klaravik.se/Terms_and_conditions.pdf). RB NL says intention alone is not sufficient and requires a VAT-equivalent deposit for third-party non-EU shippers — [RB NL, D](https://www.rbauction.eu/legal-policies/site-specific-terms/netherlands-site-specific-terms).
- **Lot-specific restrictions:** a Troostwijk 2017 Liebherr 914 with tilt-rotator was marked not suitable for export, with pick-up at Bemmel by appointment. — [Lot excerpt, S; page now 404](https://www.troostwijkauctions.com/en/l/2017-liebherr-914-litronic-radbagger-mit-kipprotator-A1-45735-1)
- **Binding bids, reserves:** Klaravik and Surplex bids are binding, and Klaravik's below-reserve bids bind for 2 working days. — [Klaravik, V](https://www.klaravik.se/Terms_and_conditions.pdf); [Surplex help, S](https://help.surplex.com/hc/en-gb/articles/32290263383185-How-can-I-bid)
- **Consumer rights do not always apply:** Troostwijk NL treats auctions as public by default, so withdrawal generally does not apply (12.1), and Klaravik's withdrawal right is only for consumers and carries depreciation charges. — [Troostwijk](https://www.troostwijkauctions.com/en/general-terms-and-conditions-netherlands); [Klaravik](https://www.klaravik.se/Terms_and_conditions.pdf)
- **Loading and transport:** Klaravik buyers handle pick-up, loading and any dismantling. — [Terms, V](https://www.klaravik.se/Terms_and_conditions.pdf). Surplex extras may include loading. — [Costs help, S](https://help.surplex.com/hc/en-gb/articles/32290390771857-What-costs-do-I-need-to-take-into-account)

### Inferences
- Budget for the premium, VAT cash flow, low-loader transport and an independent inspection before bidding, and have transport lined up before the auction closes.
- Check road-registration papers separately for a machine that will drive on public roads. The Nordics and Germany have their own rules, and auction terms address paperwork only in part, such as RB's documentation fee.
- Norwegian purchases add customs steps for EU buyers because Norway is outside the EU. The same applies to UK sales (Leeds) and Swiss sales.

### Gaps
- I found no independent data on how often auction machines turn out defective, or on the price gap between auction and dealer. Conclusions on condition risk rest on contract wording and a few anecdotes.
- Customs, CE and road-registration rules for import were not researched, as they sit outside these platforms' terms.
